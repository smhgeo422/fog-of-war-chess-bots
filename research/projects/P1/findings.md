# P1 v1 — verified toy-algorithm findings

**Status:** executable kernel and independent hosted toy/offline record verified. **Scientific strength claim: none.**

## Distinct scientific question and mechanism

See the pre-existing [Stage 0 proposal](https://github.com/smhgeo422/fog-of-war-chess-bots/blob/research/nine-independent-directions/research/projects/P1/proposal.md). The new module is [tools/stage3/P1.py](https://github.com/smhgeo422/fog-of-war-chess-bots/blob/experiment/P1-v1-toy-kernel/tools/stage3/P1.py). Given normalized valid-support posterior p, choose a strictly positive threat proposal q=(1−α)p+αr. Sample from q and estimate E_p[f] by Σ_j f(s_j)p(s_j)/q(s_j) / Σ_j p(s_j)/q(s_j).

## Preregistered toy control

α=0 posterior sampling on the same four-world support and 64-root budget.

## Measured evidence

At α=0.30, rare hidden-attack MSE decreased from 0.00021243 to 0.000011847 over 250 deterministic toy replications. Average effective sample size fell from 64.0 to 46.39, revealing the tradeoff. Exact event probability was 0.011.

## Falsification and limitation

Proposal cannot fix missing or history-invalid belief support. No held-out Brier score on actual referee histories, no chess strength.

## Required next experimental advance

Reconstruct or filter legal-history-consistent posterior states, then compare event Brier and threat recall on approved game-blocked FoW data at identical engine-root cost.

# P5 v1 — verified toy-algorithm findings

**Status:** executable kernel and independent hosted toy/offline record verified. **Scientific strength claim: none.**

## Distinct scientific question and mechanism

See the pre-existing [Stage 0 proposal](https://github.com/smhgeo422/fog-of-war-chess-bots/blob/research/nine-independent-directions/research/projects/P5/proposal.md). The new module is [tools/stage3/P5.py](https://github.com/smhgeo422/fog-of-war-chess-bots/blob/experiment/P5-v1-toy-kernel/tools/stage3/P5.py). Estimate class-specific hidden-material event probability using a shrinkage Beta prior, with separate quiet-exposed, quiet-sheltered, capture, and king-exposed classes. Penalize extra hidden-material exposure conditionally in a bounded utility, without a blanket penalty on fog captures.

## Preregistered toy control

Single pooled event probability, 400 generated training labels, 200 held-out; action class and selected move are observation-computable, target label is retrospective.

## Measured evidence

On 200 synthetic held-out examples, class-conditioned Brier = 0.147928 versus pooled 0.241875. Category Briers: capture 0.1355; king-exposed 0.1532; quiet-exposed 0.2465; quiet-sheltered 0.0565. No generic capture penalty was applied.

## Falsification and limitation

This artificial Bernoulli generator was designed to have action-class effects, so predictive superiority is expected. No actual quiet-move losses or terminal king captures from held-out game replays were scored; no calibration-to-utility proof or playing benefit.

## Required next experimental advance

Derive prospective risk labels from referee histories at the correct feature timestamp, split by opponent/game, and test incremental Brier over existing HANG and safe-first controls at identical CPU.

# P7 v1 — verified toy-algorithm findings

**Status:** executable kernel and independent hosted toy/offline record verified. **Scientific strength claim: none.**

## Distinct scientific question and mechanism

See the pre-existing [Stage 0 proposal](https://github.com/smhgeo422/fog-of-war-chess-bots/blob/research/nine-independent-directions/research/projects/P7/proposal.md). The new module is [tools/stage3/P7.py](https://github.com/smhgeo422/fog-of-war-chess-bots/blob/experiment/P7-v1-toy-kernel/tools/stage3/P7.py). A bounded ridge-regression continuation-value residual is fit only to observation-available features and a declared target, then added to a baseline value. A strict allowlist prevents selected action, opponent-policy identity and future true-board features being used online.

## Preregistered toy control

Zero residual, development-selected ridge, shuffled-label negative control. Known artificial counterfactual value target generated independently of online input.

## Measured evidence

Training 500, development 160, holdout 160. Synthetic held-out MSE = 0.0001989 versus uncorrected 0.0017069 and shuffled-target 0.0006950. Ridge 0.02; coefficients bounded and stored.

## Falsification and limitation

Targets are from a synthetic analytical teacher; not real Stockfish errors, actual future FoW rewards, or causal alternative-move values. Even a good residual MSE may not improve action choice.

## Required next experimental advance

Construct legal observation-only features with matched sampled-action rollout targets or an independent strong planner; hold out games/opponents and compare action-ranking change at equal engine cost.

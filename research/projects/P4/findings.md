# P4 v1 — verified toy-algorithm findings

**Status:** executable kernel and independent hosted toy/offline record verified. **Scientific strength claim: none.**

## Distinct scientific question and mechanism

See the pre-existing [Stage 0 proposal](https://github.com/smhgeo422/fog-of-war-chess-bots/blob/research/nine-independent-directions/research/projects/P4/proposal.md). The new module is [tools/stage3/P4.py](https://github.com/smhgeo422/fog-of-war-chess-bots/blob/experiment/P4-v1-toy-kernel/tools/stage3/P4.py). For each action a with matched direct tactical value, compare the optimal expected future continuation after grouping by hypothetical new observations with the best single precommitted continuation before seeing the observation. The difference is instrumental decision VOI, not entropy or square count.

## Preregistered toy control

Same immediate tactical score for both roots; an irrelevant observation changes visible symbols but not optimal continuation. Blind/unchanged observation is a null.

## Measured evidence

Informative view yielded exact instrumental value 0.5; irrelevant and unchanged views yielded zero. The information-only ranking selected the consequential root at equal immediate payoff.

## Falsification and limitation

A constructed continuation payoff is not the actual cost of exposing pieces to an opponent. The result does not yet validate opponent information denial or interaction with existing FOW_INFOSET (P3); adding VOI to P3 unconditionally risks double counting.

## Required next experimental advance

Generate action-conditioned next-view tables from real legal FoW transitions and show that VOI predicts changed future best moves under matched tactical/king-risk cost, including opponent-denial controls.

# P9 v1 — verified toy-algorithm findings

**Status:** executable kernel and independent hosted toy/offline record verified. **Scientific strength claim: none.**

## Distinct scientific question and mechanism

See the pre-existing [Stage 0 proposal](https://github.com/smhgeo422/fog-of-war-chess-bots/blob/research/nine-independent-directions/research/projects/P9/proposal.md). The new module is [tools/stage3/P9.py](https://github.com/smhgeo422/fog-of-war-chess-bots/blob/experiment/P9-v1-toy-kernel/tools/stage3/P9.py). Bounded-horizon AND/OR reachability over information states chooses one common own action for hidden worlds sharing an observation and universally checks every adversarial reply. An additional one-turn test enumerates legal moves and defender replies using the real local FoW referee.

## Preregistered toy control

Two hidden worlds sharing a view but requiring opposite naive captures: depth-1 cannot certify, depth-2 observation-revealing probe can. Add a losing adversarial branch to require certificate rejection.

## Measured evidence

Finite graph: depth-2 certified witness, depth-1 not winning; a losing branch invalidated the certificate. Real referee K+Q-v-K toy: two hidden king positions sharing the same white observation, 23 common possible root attempts checked, 35 full defender replies examined; **zero one-turn guaranteed captures certified**. This honest negative finding is expected for the chosen restricted horizon.

## Falsification and limitation

Real referee check found no universal one-turn action; no full 8×8 long-horizon certificate, KQ-v-K forcing policy, or new endgame theorem was obtained. The published theorem is prior work and cannot be claimed as ours.

## Required next experimental advance

Prove sound multi-turn invariants including observation-history equivalence, adversarial reply chains and repetition constraints; search tractable KQ vs K restricted classes with explicitly scoped certificates.

## P3 · Reply coverage in the existing contingent planner

The Stage 4 fixtures assumed a fixed defender reply. In the existing eBotI infoset planner, ranking replies by one-ply probability can omit a move that creates an unseen attack on our king. With the opt-in P3 v2 sub-flag, preserve the top-ranked replies and consider reallocating their last slot to the highest-ranked omitted unseen threat if the selected set lacks one. Keep the same policy score table and reply count. Leave the legacy selection as the default and use it for absent-threat, already-covered and deadline cases.

Use fixed sliding-piece and knight fixtures, their color mirrors, and negative controls. Compare root-action regret against an observation-consistent oracle over legal replies in the declared domain. Report whether the selected root changes and whether changed roots actually reduce regret. Match candidate reply and leaf slots and preserve timing/coverage caveats. This study tests the reply-coverage step before committing compute to deeper tactical search. A successful fixture does not establish a benefit at a matched live clock.
